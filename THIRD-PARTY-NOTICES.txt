CodeWalker.Core  -  https://github.com/dexyfex/CodeWalker
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Reads and writes the GTA V .yft/.ytd resource formats. The programs include
CodeWalker.Core.dll and two of its data files (strings.txt and
ShadersGen9Conversion.xml), built unmodified from the official repository at
commit 485d56bec00262ed7fa472261cce7bbc6202b96e (the git submodule at
third_party/CodeWalker).

CodeWalker.Core's FBX importer contains GPL-licensed code (Hamish Milne's
FbxWriter). It is left out of the build (third_party/Directory.Build.targets),
so none of it is in these programs.
